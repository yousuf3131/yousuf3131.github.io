    LEGS

    You walk the course in the dark with only a small lamp. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, jump with Space. On a phone, drag anywhere to move and tap JUMP.

    EYES

    You see the whole course, the fake tiles and every trap, but you can't move. Pick a ping (keys 1-5) and tap the map. Tap glowing switches to open doors, lay bridges and pause traps.

    You swap roles every level. 6 levels.
