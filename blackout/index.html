BLACKOUTSURVIVE UNTIL DAWN

One hunter with a shotgun. Everyone else has a flashlight and a dying battery. Hide in the dark, blind the hunter, and live until sunrise.

OR JOIN A FRIEND

Survivors

  • Stay alive until the clock hits zero. Dawn saves everyone still standing.
  • Your flashlight lets you see, but the hunter can see its beam from far away. Batteries run out, so grab the spares lying around.
  • Shine your light into the hunter's face to blind them for a few seconds. It works faster when friends shine too.
  • Throw your one flare to slow the hunter down. Sprinting is loud: the hunter sees where you ran.

The hunter

  • Kill every survivor before dawn. Your shotgun kills in one hit up close, but holds two shells.
  • Look for flashlight beams, and for the red ripples of anyone sprinting. Scream to see everyone for a moment.
  • Don't look into the lights.
Everyone gets a turn as the hunter. Points for surviving, stuns and kills decide the winner.
