Why SafeClip

Other clippers fail quietly.
SafeClip doesn't.

You click save, close the tab, and the page never shows up. It's the most common complaint about Notion clippers. SafeClip is built around fixing it.

01

Kept on your computer first

Every clip goes into a local queue before anything is sent, so closing the tab or losing Wi-Fi can't lose it.

Clip→Queue→Notion
02

Retried until it lands

Offline, rate limited, or Notion having a bad day? SafeClip waits and tries again on its own, and shows you anything still waiting.

Failed→Retry→Saved
03

Saved even when something's off

A broken image becomes a link. A renamed column gets its value written into the page. The clip still goes through.

FAQ

Questions

What happens if I'm offline when I clip?

The clip is stored on your computer and sent automatically when you're back online. You can see anything still waiting in SafeClip's settings.

Do you see my pages or my Notion data?

No. Clips go straight from your browser to Notion's official API. SafeClip has no analytics, no tracking and no server that receives your pages. Privacy policy.

Do I need a Notion database?

No. You can save into any normal page. If you want tags and a reading status, SafeClip can create a Reading list database for you in one click.

How do I add a page or database later?

Open it in Notion and pick ··· → Connections → SafeClip, then press refresh in SafeClip.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers, installed from the Chrome Web Store.

What counts as a clip?

Each page, selection or link you save is one clip. The free plan includes 30 a month and resets on the 1st. Pro is unlimited.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open SafeClip's settings and choose Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Problems? Email yousufsak99@gmail.com.
