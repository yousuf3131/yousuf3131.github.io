Never lose a clip.

SafeClip is a Chrome extension that saves web pages into your Notion databases. Every clip is kept on your computer until Notion confirms it, and retried automatically if anything goes wrong.

Add to Chrome

What it does

  • Save the full article, your highlighted text, or just the link
  • Pick a database and fill in tags, status, dates and other properties as you clip
  • Quick save with Alt+Shift+S or right-click
  • Offline or Notion down? Clips wait in a queue and send when they can
