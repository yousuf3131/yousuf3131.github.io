Privacy policy

SafeClip – Save to Notion · Last updated November 2026

What SafeClip does with your data

When you clip a page, SafeClip reads that page in your browser and sends the content you chose (title, link, text, images, highlighted text and your note) directly from your browser to Notion's API, into the database you picked. It only reads a page when you click the button, press the shortcut, or use the right-click menu.

What is stored

Your Notion access token, your database list, your last-used choices, and clips waiting to be sent are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage). Clips are removed from the waiting list once they reach Notion; a short history of recent saves (title, link, database) is kept locally so you can open them.

What is not collected

SafeClip has no analytics, no tracking, and no server that receives your pages. We don't sell or share data.

Third parties

Notion receives the clips you send, under Notion's own privacy policy. Sign-in: when you connect with "Connect Notion", a one-time code is exchanged for an access token through a small sign-in service that holds SafeClip's Notion app secret; it does not store your token. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe; SafeClip never sees your card details.

Removing your data

Click Disconnect in SafeClip's settings, or uninstall the extension, to delete everything stored locally. You can also revoke access any time in Notion under Settings → Connections.
